# Policy documents (seeded verbatim)

Create one file per document in `backend/seed/policies/<slug>.md` with exactly this text.
The seed splits each document into passages: **one passage per bullet** (paragraphs
without bullets are one passage each). Passages are quoted verbatim in the UI, so keep
them short and self-contained. These documents must stay consistent with
`business-rules.md`; if you change a number there, change it here too.

Credit unions in the seed: `7` = Riverbend Credit Union, `9` = Lakeside Community Credit
Union. Both use these policies.

## fee-refund-policy — Fee Refund Policy
- Members in good standing may receive up to 3 fee refunds in any 12-month period, across all of their accounts.
- Refunds of any fee type count toward the 3-refund limit.
- An overdraft fee qualifies for a refund when a deposit posted on the same day as the fee would have covered the payment that caused it, had it been processed first.
- The member must ask for the refund within 60 days of the date the fee was charged.
- The refund is always the exact amount of the fee. We never refund more than the fee.
- Each fee can be refunded only once.
- Overdraft fees and returned payment fees are covered by this policy. Other fees are not covered and may be refunded at staff discretion.
- Exceptions to this policy, such as a refund beyond the 3-refund limit, must be approved by a supervisor.

## fee-schedule — Fee Schedule
- Overdraft fee (Courtesy Pay): $35.00 per item paid when the balance is not enough.
- Returned payment fee (NSF): $35.00 per item returned unpaid.
- Out-of-network ATM fee: $5.00 per withdrawal at an ATM outside our network.
- Savings withdrawal fee: $5.00 for each withdrawal from savings beyond 6 in a month.
- Paper statement fee: $2.00 per month for mailed statements.

## overdraft-program — Overdraft Program (Courtesy Pay)
- Courtesy Pay is our overdraft program. When a debit card payment, check or bill payment would overdraw the account, we may pay it instead of declining it.
- Each item we pay this way is charged the overdraft fee listed in the Fee Schedule.
- Members can opt out of Courtesy Pay for debit card payments at any time.
- An overdrawn balance must be brought back above $0 within 30 days.

## posting-order — How Transactions Are Processed
- We process each day's transactions overnight, after the business day ends.
- Within a day, transactions are processed in a set order and each one gets a posting reference showing its position in that order.
- Card payments and bill payments may be processed before direct deposits that arrive the same day. When that happens, the balance can go below $0 for a moment, even though the deposit arrived that day.
- When a same-day deposit is processed after a payment and an overdraft fee results, the member may ask for a refund under the Fee Refund Policy.

## good-standing — Member Good Standing
- A member is in good standing when they have no debt in collections and no history of fraud on any account.
- A debt stops counting against good standing once it is paid or resolved.
- A history of fraud is permanent for the purpose of fee refunds.
- Members who are not in good standing are not eligible for fee refunds under the Fee Refund Policy.

## approval-authority — Staff Approval Authority
- Member support staff may approve a fee refund of up to $50.00 when it follows the Fee Refund Policy.
- Refunds above $50.00, and any exception to the Fee Refund Policy, must be approved by a supervisor.
- Refunds may be issued automatically for clear overdraft fee cases when the member keeps at least one refund available afterward.
- Every refund is recorded with who approved it, when, and why.

## member-communication — Member Communication Guidelines
- Reply in the language the member wrote in.
- Use plain words. Say "overdraft fee", not internal program names, codes or reference numbers.
- Greet the member by first name and keep replies short.
- When we refund a fee, say so clearly and tell the member the money is back in their account today.
- When we can't refund a fee, explain the reason kindly in one sentence and offer to help with anything else.
- Never share another member's information or internal notes.
