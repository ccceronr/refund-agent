# Staff Approval Authority

- Member support staff may approve a fee refund of up to $50.00 when it follows the Fee Refund Policy.
- Refunds above $50.00, and any exception to the Fee Refund Policy, must be approved by a supervisor.
- Refunds may be issued automatically for clear overdraft fee cases when the member keeps at least one refund available afterward.
- Every refund is recorded with who approved it, when, and why.
