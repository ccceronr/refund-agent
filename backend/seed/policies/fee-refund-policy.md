# Fee Refund Policy

- Members in good standing may receive up to 3 fee refunds in any 12-month period, across all of their accounts.
- Refunds of any fee type count toward the 3-refund limit.
- An overdraft fee qualifies for a refund when a deposit posted on the same day as the fee would have covered the payment that caused it, had it been processed first.
- The member must ask for the refund within 60 days of the date the fee was charged.
- The refund is always the exact amount of the fee. We never refund more than the fee.
- Each fee can be refunded only once.
- Overdraft fees and returned payment fees are covered by this policy. Other fees are not covered and may be refunded at staff discretion.
- Exceptions to this policy, such as a refund beyond the 3-refund limit, must be approved by a supervisor.
