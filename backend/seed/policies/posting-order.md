# How Transactions Are Processed

- We process each day's transactions overnight, after the business day ends.
- Within a day, transactions are processed in a set order and each one gets a posting reference showing its position in that order.
- Card payments and bill payments may be processed before direct deposits that arrive the same day. When that happens, the balance can go below $0 for a moment, even though the deposit arrived that day.
- When a same-day deposit is processed after a payment and an overdraft fee results, the member may ask for a refund under the Fee Refund Policy.
